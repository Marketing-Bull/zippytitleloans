FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is hard money lending?

Hard money lending refers to a type of short-term, asset-based lending typically used in real estate transactions. Unlike traditional bank loans, where a borrower’s creditworthiness and financial history are primary considerations, hard money loans are primarily secured by the value of a specific real estate asset, such as a property or land.

How do I apply for a hard money loan with Zippy?

Just fill out the form by clicking on the Apply Now button.

What are the eligibility requirements for a hard money loan?

Every circumstance is different, in general we look for 30% equity and a realistic ARV. Your experience as an investor may play a role and affect the rates.

What types of real estate investments do you fund?

We primarily fund single and multi-family homes in Florida which are not being used as a primary residence.

What are the interest rates and terms for your loans?

Interest rates and terms vary with market conditions and specific deal points. In general our rates are 11% to 13% with durations from 3 months to 2 years.

How quickly can I get approved and receive funding?

You can be approved in as little as 24 hours. However, sometimes due diligence takes longer depending whether an appraisal or inspection is required.

Are there any upfront fees or hidden costs?

There are no hidden costs or fees. We may charge a $750+ application fee to cover title search and attorney fees.

How is Zippy different from other hard money lenders?

Zippy is a modern, tech forward solution for real estate investors. We aim to simplify and speed up the process of acquiring a loan. We do not think in terms of singular transactions, but rather as your long term partner.
