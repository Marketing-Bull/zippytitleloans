About us

We are Zippy

We’re young, we’re fun, and we know what you want. We are on a mission to bring effortless, simple financing to Florida’s real estate investors.

The Mission

Empowering Florida real estate investors with simple, secure, and fast financing solutions is our mission at Zippy. We’re here to transform the lending experience, making it efficient, transparent, and enjoyable. We’re not just a lender; we’re your trusted partner for success.

The Vision

To be the foremost catalyst for real estate investment success in Florida by continually innovating and providing the most convenient, secure, and efficient financing solutions. We envision a future where every real estate investor seamlessly accesses the resources they need, backed by a trusted partnership that empowers their growth and prosperity.
